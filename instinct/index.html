The purpose of the paper run

Collect the evidence first, then analyse what would have worked. This is a research journal, not a fixed entry-and-exit strategy or a list of guaranteed winners.

  1. Start with the overnight-news idea

    Use the 554-stock universe (Nifty 500 plus the watchlist) and news published from 4:00 PM on the previous trading day to 8:30 AM on the session day, in IST. Weekends and market holidays in between belong to the same window. Analyse the news and sentiment, and record exactly five hypothetical paper scenarios per day. Direction is a hypothesis, not a known outcome.

  2. Preserve the view before the outcome Proposed logging detail

    Save the news links, publication and capture times, analysis timestamp, sentiment, expected direction, reasoning and uncertainty. Keep the full candidate set, including stocks not shortlisted, with selection or exclusion reasons. Don't silently rewrite the pre-open view after prices move.

  3. Keep the price path, not just the closing colour Proposed logging detail

    Record time-stamped price observations after the open, with the source, interval and any gaps. Separate the overnight opening gap from movement after a possible entry. A green close alone does not establish that a trade was available or profitable.

  4. Analyse alternatives after the session Proposed analysis

    Compare candidate selection, entry timing, exits and price-confirmation ideas using the recorded path. For each paper scenario, show entry and exit times and prices, sizing assumptions, gross result, costs, slippage and net result. Keep skipped cases and losses visible. Label retrospective scenarios as retrospective, not decisions made in advance.

  5. Carry discoveries into a later test Proposed validation

    Patterns found by looking back are new hypotheses. Record each change and test it on subsequent, unseen sessions. Separate exploratory results from forward results; paper performance is not proof of real-world returns.

Still open: news and price sources, the universe snapshot, holiday/weekend handling, capture frequency, scoring method, selection limits, entry/exit comparisons and cost assumptions. These are not agreed trading rules. No schedule or automated collection is running.

Proposed record layout

One date. The whole trail.

A checklist for future session records, not a claim that this data is already available.

01 · Session & source coverage
  • Session date, IST timestamps and the 554-stock universe snapshot used.
  • Exact news-window bounds, sources checked, collection time and known missing coverage. No news found is different from news not checked.
  • Record version and any later correction, preserving the earlier analysis.
02 · News, analysis & candidates
  • Stock identifier, headline, original link, publication time, capture time and duplicate/event grouping.
  • Sentiment, expected direction, catalyst, reasoning, uncertainty and analysis time.
  • Shortlisted and unselected candidates, ranking if used, and reasons. Keep no-selection sessions too.
03 · Prices & paper scenarios
  • Time-stamped prices, data source, observation interval, adjustments and missing observations.
  • Scenario name; whether defined before or after the session; entry/exit conditions, times, prices and quantities.
  • Gross result, itemised cost assumptions, slippage and net result. Missing data stays missing, not zero.
04 · Review & next hypotheses
  • What happened versus the pre-open view, including unselected stocks, losses and skipped cases.
  • Which comparisons were tried, what appeared to work and the limits of the available sample.
  • New hypotheses and methodology changes, with the date from which a later forward test would apply.

Paper research only. No broker connection, real orders, live signals or performance claims. This public journal excludes private account and capital details.
